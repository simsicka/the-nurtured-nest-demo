THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to Know

Booking & Payment

  • A 50% deposit is due at booking to reserve the date and time.
  • The remaining balance is due 7 days before the event.
  • Final guest count is due 7 days before the event.
  • Additional children beyond the package limit require approval and a per-child fee.

Cancellations

  • Cancel at least 7 days before the event for a full deposit refund.
  • Cancellations within 7 days are nonrefundable, but the deposit may be transferred once to a new available date for illness or emergencies.

Safety & Supervision

  • Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times.
  • No drop-off childcare is provided.
  • A signed waiver is required for every participating child.
  • The Nurtured Nest reserves the right to pause or end play if safety becomes a concern.

Food & Decor

  • Outside food, cake, and non-alcoholic drinks are welcome.
  • Families are responsible for allergy and dietary needs.
  • Decor must be approved in advance.
  • No glitter, confetti, silly string, or open flames.

Party Availability

  • Weekend parties begin at 1:00 PM.
  • Weekday evenings are available by request, based on schedule and availability.
