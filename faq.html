A LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU COME

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details for a comfortable first visit.

Classes

Does my child attend on their own?

A parent or caregiver stays on site and with their child for classes, open play, parties and events unless a program is clearly advertised as drop-off. The Nurtured Nest is an enrichment space, not childcare.

What ages are welcome?

Programs serve babies from 6 months through children 11 and up. Individual classes list their age ranges. The Merry & Bright parent guide includes series-specific age groups.

How large are classes?

Weekend classes are capped at 5 children. Other series list their own caps. Waitlists may be available.

What if we arrive late?

You may come in when you arrive, but class still ends on time. Arriving a few minutes early is encouraged.

Accessibility & Support

Can you support different needs?

The Nest welcomes children with a wide range of developmental, sensory, communication and accessibility needs. Please communicate helpful supports before class.

Are classes therapy?

Classes are enrichment: play, music, movement, art and connection. Speech-language therapy is offered separately at The Nest by Katherine Vergel, M.A., CCC-SLP, under her own agreement.

Safety & Health

When should we stay home?

Please stay home with fever, vomiting, diarrhea, unexplained rash or anything contagious.

Is the space allergen-free?

Classes may involve paint, dough, sand, water, bubbles, plants and snacks. The space cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment. Please disclose allergies when booking.

Which agreements are required?

Before your first visit, the required agreements are the Liability Waiver, Parent/Caregiver Policies Agreement, and Photo & Media Consent. Declining photo consent does not affect participation. Complete the Online Waiver ↗

Memberships & Payment

What does Saturday Enrichment cost?

$25 per class, or $100 for a 4-week upfront Saturday Enrichment Pass. The pass guarantees your child's spot for the entire month. Class sizes are very limited.

How does the 5 Credit Package work?

The package is $125. Credits are valid for 3 months after purchase and may be used for Baby Sensory, The Music and Me, Preschool Prep Through Play, Get Your Wiggles Out, and Play Crew.

How much is Open Gym?

Open Gym is $15 on select days. It may be prepaid or paid via Zelle to thenurturednestfl@outlook.com.

Do you accept Step Up For Students?

Some programs accept Step Up For Students, as noted on each program. Contact us about the program you are interested in; eligibility is program-specific.

Parties & Hosting

When are birthday parties available?

Weekend parties start at 1:00 PM. Weekday evenings are available by request, based on schedule and availability.

How do party payments work?

A 50% deposit holds the date. The balance and final guest count are due 7 days before the party.

May we bring food and decorations?

Outside food and cake are welcome. Decor must be approved in advance. No glitter, confetti, silly string or open flames.

Can I teach a class or host a pop-up?

We welcome inquiries from people interested in teaching a class or hosting a pop-up. Proof of liability insurance is required. Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee booking. Send a General Interest Inquiry ↗

Location & General Information

Where are you located?

23123 State Road 7, Suite 250, Boca Raton, FL 33428. Park in the large parking lot, take the elevator to the second floor, turn left down the hallway, and The Nurtured Nest is at the end.

What are the hours?

Programming begins at 9:30 AM. End times vary by day and program. Contact us for your program's timing.

How can I contact the team?

Email thenurturednestfl@outlook.com or call 689-233-5144. For provider-specific questions, contact the team to be connected with the appropriate provider.
